Frontier 0.2.0: a fresh start for the settlement mode on Minecraft Java 26.4 Snapshot 3.

This is a rebuild. 0.1.x settlements are not carried over: start a new Frontier world.

- Place and light a campfire to found your settlement. Your land starts at 3x3 chunks around it.
- A pioneer arrives within two minutes and needs no bed. If everyone is ever lost, a new pioneer comes.
- Later settlers each need a sheltered bed (solid roof 2-6 blocks above) inside your land. New beds count as soon as you place them.
- Every 5 residents unlock one more ring of chunks. Land never shrinks, even if settlers die or leave.
- Settlers eat real food from your stockpile; hungry, less loyal settlers leave; loyal ones can starve.
- /frontier status explains what the settlement is waiting for. /frontier settlers [all] lists everyone.
- Look at a chest or barrel and use /frontier stockpile; look at your own bed and use /frontier keepbed.
- /frontier retire removes a settler who is lost; /frontier reset, then reset confirm, starts over without touching your buildings.

Requires H.O.W.L. 0.0.45+ and CodaLauncher Nightly. Choose Frontier, then Nightly Play / Install-Repair.
