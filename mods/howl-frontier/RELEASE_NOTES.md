Frontier 0.2.2: easier-to-read chat.

All Frontier messages now have a consistent style, and colour carries meaning:
- Gold headings; gray labels with white values; dark-gray separators between fields.
- Green for good news and confirmations, yellow for waiting or needs attention, red for stopped, failed or refused.
- /frontier status is split into short lines: settlement, land, settlers, beds, arrivals, stockpile.
- /frontier settlers colours each settler's state, food and loyalty (green, yellow, red) and highlights IDs you can type back.
- Announcements (founded, arriving, joined, leaving, died, land grew) use the same colours.

The words are unchanged, so nothing depends on colour alone. Includes the 0.2.1 arrival fix.

Requires H.O.W.L. 0.0.45+ and CodaLauncher Nightly. Choose Frontier, then Nightly Play / Install-Repair.
